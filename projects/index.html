Improving DonkeyCar CNN Robustness with Data Augmentation

UC San Diego | DSC 190 Team Project | Summer 2026

Worked on a three-person team investigating whether an autonomous vehicle's convolutional neural network (CNN), trained on midday camera images, could generalize to morning and nighttime driving.

  • The team extended DonkeyCar's image augmentation pipeline using Albumentations to address differences in lighting, color, contrast, and motion blur.
  • Compared baseline and augmented models across morning, midday, and nighttime conditions. The strongest models maintained midday performance and partially generalized to nighttime driving.
  • Used Claude Code to help analyze driving images and nvestigate failure modes, including background interference and the effects of image cropping.

Tools: Python, DonkeyCar, Albumentations, Claude Code
